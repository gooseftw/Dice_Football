How it works

40-yard field, numbered like a real field up to the 20 from each end. Touchdown = 7. No PAT. First-down lines every 10 yards. 4 downs to reach the next line. Each play: Offense 2d6, Defense 1d6.

Standard: yards = O1 + O2 − D
Big play: both offense 5–6 and defense 1–2 → standard yards + one bonus d6
To the house: 6–6 and defense 1 → offense touchdown from anywhere (+7)
Stuffed: defense die is greater than both offense dice → 0 yards
Sack: defense die > sum of offense dice → lose twice the difference. Through your own goal line is a safety.
Turnover: both offense 1–2 and defense 5–6 → other team takes over at that spot
Pick 6: 1–1 and defense 6 → defense touchdown (+7)
Priority: Pick 6 → Turnover → Sack → Stuffed → To the house → Big play → Standard.

New drives after a TD or to start the game: own 10. Punt on 4th pins the opponent at their 3. Safety = 2; the other team then starts at their 10.

Win: 8 possessions each. A possession ends on a touchdown, safety, punt, turnover, or turnover on downs. If the score is tied after 8 and 8, each team gets one extra possession (Away, then Home). Repeat that pair until one team leads after both have gone. If a team is about to start a possession already ahead by more than the other team can score with their remaining possessions (7 per possession), the game ends.
